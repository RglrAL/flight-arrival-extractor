    Help
    1. Upload one or more “Passenger Arrival Details” PDFs. Each file is read on this device and its rows are checked against the PDF's own passenger total.
    2. Select the arrival date. Only passengers whose own row carries that date are included — never the report date or a neighbouring row.
    3. Review arrivals. Flights are grouped and sorted by time. Anything uncertain appears in the review panel instead of being guessed. Click a passenger to see their original row highlighted in the source PDF.
    4. Print or export. “Print schedule” gives the clean list; “Print sheets + summary” reprints each original report with its day-summary added at the top; Excel/CSV exports carry the full row data.

    Scanned or image-only PDFs are rejected with a clear message rather than guessed — V1 requires PDFs with a text layer.
    About

    Flight Arrival Extractor builds consolidated arrival schedules from tour operators' Passenger Arrival Details reports (Trafalgar, Insight, Brendan, Costsaver…).

    Privacy: all processing happens in your browser. Passenger PDFs are never uploaded, stored or transmitted — closing the tab removes everything.

    Accuracy: table rows are reconstructed from the printed layout's coordinates, so a passenger can never inherit a flight, time or date from an adjacent row. Extracted counts are cross-checked against each PDF's own stated total, and anything uncertain is flagged for review.

    Works offline once loaded. Use your browser's “Install app” / “Add to Dock” to keep it in easy reach.
